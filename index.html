Questions.

Can the other person see Cue?

Not on your camera. If you share your screen or the tab Cue is in, they will see the panel.

How accurate are the answers?

They are drafts based on what was said and the context you add. Cue can mishear or be wrong, so read before you speak.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Cue understands English, Hindi and Hinglish and answers in the language you were asked.

Can I get a refund?

Minutes never expire, and a session stopped in its first minute is refunded automatically. For payment refunds, see the Refund Policy.

Can I use Cue in a real interview?

Only where the people running it allow AI help. Many interviews do not, so check first.

How do I install it?

Download the zip, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and click Load unpacked. Choose the unzipped folder and the free demo opens.

What happens to my data?

Nothing is heard until you press Start. Transcripts stay in your browser unless you choose to save them. See the Privacy Policy.
