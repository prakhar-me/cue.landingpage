How it works

Three steps.

No chatbot to type into during the call. Cue waits until someone asks you something.

Add your context

Upload your resume, paste the job description and list your projects. Answers use your real experience.

Start on your call

Open Meet, Zoom or Teams in Chrome, click the Cue icon, pick the length and press Start.

Glance and speak

When a question ends, a suggested answer streams into the panel. Say it in your own words.

Features

Everything in one small panel.

Drag it, resize it, set its opacity or shrink it to a dot.

Hears the call

Listens to the other side of the call and spots when a question is finished, so it does not fire on every word.

English and Hindi

Understands both, including Hinglish, and answers in the language the question was asked in.

Coding questions

"Write a function to…" gets working code, the approach, and time and space complexity.

Three answer styles

Short to say right away, Detailed for 30 to 60 seconds, or STAR for behavioral questions.

Simple English

Switch to short sentences and everyday words, so answers sound like you talking.

Interview and meeting modes

Meeting mode suggests responses, recommendations and action items instead of interview answers.

Type to ask

Ask Cue anything yourself, right from the panel.

Notes after the call

Turn on transcript saving to get a summary, the questions asked and follow-ups.

Pause any time

Pause or stop from the panel. Paused time is free.

Privacy

Nothing starts until you press Start.

  • Cue hears the call only during a session you start, and shows when it is listening
  • Live transcripts stay in your browser's memory and are dropped when you stop
  • Saving transcripts is off until you turn it on
  • Your resume and job description stay in your browser and are sent only to generate an answer
  • One button deletes everything stored

Use Cue only where AI help and recording are allowed. Many interviews and exams do not allow it, and some places need everyone's consent to record.

FAQ

Questions.

Which apps does it work with?

Google Meet, Zoom on the web and Microsoft Teams on the web, in Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop.

How do I install it?

Download the zip and unzip it. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Cue opens a welcome page with the free demo.

How does the free trial work?

After you install Cue, a welcome page opens with a 6 minute demo. A sample interviewer asks questions and you see Cue answer them. No card needed.

Do credits expire?

No. Buy a pack once and use it across your whole job hunt. Credits are spent in 6 minute steps only while a session is running.

Will it make things up about me?

It is told to use only the resume, job description and projects you give it. If something is missing, it suggests a general approach and tells you what to add.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Cue understands English, Hindi and Hinglish, and answers in the language of the question. You can also fix it to English only or Hindi only in Settings.

How do I pay?

Through Razorpay, by UPI, card, netbanking or wallet. Cue never sees your card or UPI details.
