How it works

Three steps.

No chatbot to type into during the call. Cue waits until someone asks you something.

Add your context

Upload your resume, paste the job description or meeting notes, and list your projects. Answers use your real experience.

Start on your call

Open Meet, Zoom or Teams in Chrome, click the Cue icon, pick the length and press Start.

Glance and speak

When a question ends, a suggested answer streams into the panel. Say it in your own words.

Features

Everything in one small panel.

Drag it, resize it, set its opacity or shrink it to a dot.

Hears the call

Listens to the other side of the call and waits until a question is finished, so it does not fire on every word.

English, Hindi and Hinglish

Understands all three and answers in the language the question was asked in.

Meeting mode

Suggests a response, a recommendation or an action item, depending on what was asked.

Interview practice

Short, Detailed or STAR answers, built from your resume and the job description.

Coding questions

"Write a function to…" gets working code, the approach, and time and space complexity.

Simple English

Switch to short sentences and everyday words, so answers sound like you talking.

Type to ask

Ask Cue anything yourself, right from the panel.

Notes after the call

Turn on transcript saving to get a summary, decisions and follow-ups.

Pause any time

Pause or stop from the panel. Paused time is free.

FAQ

Questions.

Can the other person see Cue? What happens when I share my screen?

Cue is a small panel inside your Chrome tab. It is not part of your camera video, so people on the call do not see it there. If you share your whole screen, or the tab Cue is in, they will see the panel. Cue does not hide itself from screen sharing. [CONFIRM]

What if my internet drops during a session?

Cue needs a connection to hear the call and send answers, so it pauses while you are offline and carries on when you are back. If you stay offline for about 3 minutes, the session ends and you are only charged for the 6 minute steps already started. [CONFIRM]

Can I get a refund?

Unused minutes never expire, so they stay in your balance for next time. If you stop a session in its first minute before Cue answers anything, that minute comes back automatically. For refunds of a payment, see the Refund and Cancellation Policy. [CONFIRM: refund window and how refunds are paid]

How accurate are the answers?

Answers are drafts based on what was said and what you give Cue: your resume, job description and projects. Cue can mishear names or technical terms and it can be wrong, so read an answer before you use it. [CONFIRM]

Which apps does it work with?

Google Meet, Zoom on the web and Microsoft Teams on the web, in Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop.

How do I install it?

Download the zip and unzip it. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Cue then opens a welcome page with the free demo.

How does the free trial work?

After you install Cue, a welcome page opens with 12 free minutes. A sample interviewer asks questions and you see Cue answer them. You can also type your own. No card needed.

Do minutes expire?

No. Buy a pack once and use it whenever you need it. Minutes are only used while a session is running, in 6 minute steps, and paused time is free.

Will it make things up about me?

It is told to use only the resume, job description and projects you give it. If something is missing, it suggests a general approach and tells you what to add.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Cue understands English, Hindi and Hinglish, and answers in the language of the question. You can also fix it to English only or Hindi only in Settings.

How do I pay?

Through Razorpay, by UPI, card, netbanking or wallet. Cue never sees your card or UPI details.

Can I use Cue in a real interview?

Only where the people running it allow AI help. Many interviews and exams do not, and some places need everyone's consent before a call is recorded or transcribed. Check first and follow the rules. See the Terms of Service.
