Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what Cue ("we"), Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, collects when you use Cue, why, and the choices you have.

What stays in your browser

  • Your interview or meeting context: role, company, job description, resume text, projects and profile.
  • Your settings, and past session titles and lengths.
  • Transcripts of past sessions, only if you turn on "Save transcripts". This is off by default.

Settings and privacy has a button that deletes all of this from your browser.

What we receive and why

  • Account: a random account key. We store only a hash of it, with your minute balance, purchases and session times, to run billing.
  • Call audio, during a session you start: short audio clips of the other side of the call (and your microphone, if you choose "Call + mic") are sent to our server and on to our speech-to-text provider to turn them into text. We do not store the audio.
  • Questions and context: to suggest an answer, we send the question, the last few lines of the conversation and your context to our AI provider. We do not store this text or log transcripts on our server.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes payments. We receive the order and payment IDs and the amount, not your card or UPI details.
  • Website analytics: none. Our website does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Service providers

  • Groq, for speech to text and AI answers.
  • Render, which hosts our server.
  • Razorpay, for payments.

Providers process data only to run Cue for us, under their own terms.

How long we keep data

Account and balance records are kept while your account is in use. Purchase records are kept for up to 8 years, as needed for tax and accounting. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

You can delete local data from Settings and privacy at any time. To access or delete the account data we hold, write to support.cue@gmail.com with your recovery key. We reply within 1 to 2 hours and complete requests within 7 days.

Grievance officer
